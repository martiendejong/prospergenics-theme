Martien de Jong

"I'm building Jengo, our AI agent platform. It runs most of what we ship."

The story

Martien has been building software professionally since 2006: hospital systems, government platforms, rail apps, SEO automation. Somewhere along the way the question shifted from "what can I build" to "how is value actually created". ProsperGenics is his answer in progress: a community spanning the Netherlands and Kenya where people create value for themselves and each other, with software as the lever. Today he builds Jengo, the AI agent platform that runs most of what the community ships.
